Implement "Glitch", Motion Prompt #023.

What the sample shows: Text slices shift sideways, colours split and small bars appear, with no brightness flashing.

Motion spec:
- White bold text (104 px). It glitches twice per 4.7 s: 0.55–1.25 s (5 steps) and 2.9–3.74 s (6 steps); otherwise it is still.
- Each step lasts 0.14 s and shows one fixed disturbance (stepped, not smoothly animated).
- Colour split: a coral copy shifted 7–16 px left and 2 px down, a cyan (#22D3EE) copy shifted the same amount right and 2 px up, with the white text on top.
- Slice shift: 2–4 horizontal strips (10–35 px tall, y 40–150) are displaced 18–64 px to the left or right.
- Three small colour bars (coral, cyan, yellow; 30–120 wide × 5–12 tall) at random positions.
- Background brightness never changes (no full-frame flashing). The random values use a fixed seed.

When to use it: Use it for a digital, error or uneasy feel, as an accent on titles and scene changes.
