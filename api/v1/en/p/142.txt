Implement "Shatter", Motion Prompt #142.

What the sample shows: The picture cracks like glass, the shards fly off and the next scene is revealed.

Motion spec:
- Mountain scene → day scene → mountain, 2.35 s each. The impact point is (304, 92).
- Cracks: 0.3–0.65 s. Thirteen radial lines and rings at radii of about 40, 98 and 175 (each ±15%) appear in order of distance from the centre (cubic ease-out, out to 420 px). A white flash star at the centre fades by 0.6 s.
- The glass breaks at 0.82 s: 52 shards (13 × 4 rings) cut from the old scene fly outward at 220–480 px/s (vertical speed × 0.7, plus up to 120 upward), with gravity 1300 px/s², spinning ±4 rad/s and shrinking up to 35% over 1 s.
- Outer rings leave later (0.035 s per ring plus up to 0.03 s at random). Each shard fades out 0.55–1.0 s after it starts moving.
- Shards have a white 2.5 px edge; the next scene is underneath.

When to use it: Use it for an impact, a break or a twist, just before a title or a result that should hit hard.
