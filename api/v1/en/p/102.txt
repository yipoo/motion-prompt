What the sample shows: A light in front of the face and high above comes on; the face lights evenly on both sides and only a small symmetrical shadow falls under the chin.

- Head: entirely lit #FFC75A (no shadow side)
- Shadow under the chin: an ellipse centred at y 78+1.06R, 0.72R wide and 0.38R tall (R = 29, about 21×11 px), colour #33405A
