Implement "Tilt", Motion Prompt #026.

What the sample shows: The camera stays in place and pivots upward, from a person at ground level up to the top of a tower.

Motion spec:
- Camera: position fixed (height 1.4 m); pitch only, -0.06 rad → +0.36 rad (-3° → +21°, about 24° in total)
- Timing: 0.5 s to 3.4 s (2.9 s), cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1))
- Subject: a person stands still 7 m ahead; a 9.5 m tower (antenna tip at 11.8 m) stands 26 m away behind
- On screen everything slides downward: the person leaves at the bottom while the tower top and its antenna enter
- Return: hold 3.4–4.2 s, then cross-fade back to the start over the last 0.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s)
- Top-right readout shows the angle in whole degrees (-3° → +21°)

When to use it: To show the height or scale of a building or a person.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
