Implement "Lazy loading", Motion Prompt #090.

What the sample shows: As grey placeholders scroll into view, a spinner turns in each, and the images fade in as they arrive. The top row is loaded from the start.

Motion spec:
- A 3 × 3 grid of 151 × 92 placeholders; the first row is shown from the start. Scroll: 0→110 px at 0.35–1.1 s, 110→225 px at 1.7–2.5 s, 225→0 at 3.5–4.5 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- Loading starts when a placeholder's top edge passes above the bottom of the viewport (168 px), with each column 0.12 s later than the one to its left.
- For 0.45 s a spinner (radius 11, one turn per 0.8 s) turns in the placeholder's centre.
- Then the image fades in 0→1 over 0.35 s (cubic ease-out). Unloaded placeholders show an image icon.
- Placeholders that leave the bottom of the view are reset out of sight.

When to use it: Use it on image-heavy pages to make the first view load faster.
