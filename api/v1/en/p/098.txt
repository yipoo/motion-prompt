Implement "Rim light", Motion Prompt #098.

What the sample shows: The surroundings go dark and two lights behind the person, left and right, draw thin bright edges only along both sides of the head and shoulders; the plan at the bottom right shows where the two lights are.

Motion spec:
- Scene 1: a head-and-shoulders figure in front of a horizon at y 150 (head centre x 270, y 78, radius 29 px, no face or hair). 
- Background: light scene → dark scene (sky #2B2D33, ground #24252A); figure #4D5A75 → #1F2128
- Bright edges: two copies of the figure in amber #FFC75A, shifted 3.5 px left and right and 2.5 px up, placed behind the dark figure (3.5 px wide edges)
- Plan at the bottom right (66×66 px): lights at ±135° seen from above (behind, left and right); top-right readout "±135°"
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
