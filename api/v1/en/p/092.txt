Implement "Page transition", Motion Prompt #092.

What the sample shows: Clicking a card in the work list sweeps an orange panel across from the left; the URL changes and the panel exits to the right on the detail page. "Back to list" returns the same way.

Motion spec:
- Click on a card at 0.8 s. At 0.9–1.3 s a panel (orange, full viewport, logo in the centre) enters from the left and covers everything (cubic ease-in-out).
- At 1.3 s, while covered, the page and the URL in the address bar (/works → /works/02) switch.
- At 1.4–1.85 s the panel exits to the right (cubic ease-in-out).
- Click on "← Back to list" at 3.0 s: the panel enters at 3.1–3.5 s, the page switches at 3.5 s and the panel exits at 3.6–4.05 s.
- The panel covers the whole viewport, header included.

When to use it: Use it on brand sites and portfolios to link one page to the next.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 539 × 208 px; loop: 4.8 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
