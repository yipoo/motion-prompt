Implement "Filter chips", Motion Prompt #061.

What the sample shows: Clicking the "New" and "Free" chips adds a check mark and turns them blue, and cards that do not match fade out.

Motion spec:
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. Four chips ("All, New, Popular, Free" in Japanese): 34 tall, radius 17, width = text + 32, 10 px apart from the left.
- Clicks: New on at 0.95 s, Free on at 1.75 s, New off at 3.0 s, Free off at 3.6 s.
- Each change takes 0.15 s with easeOutCubic: the chip widens 18 px to make room for a check mark; fill light blue, border and text blue.
- List below: four cards (242×54, two columns, two rows); cards that do not match drop from opacity 1 to 0.12.

When to use it: Use it to let people narrow a list or search results by combining a few quick filters.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 542 × 191 px; loop: 4.5 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
