Implement "Stagger", Motion Prompt #002.

What the sample shows: Chart bars rise one after another from the left, each slightly delayed.

Motion spec:
- One pass per 2.35 s. Seven bars (width 44, gap 20, heights 52, 84, 68, 112, 92, 136, 158), coloured blue, coral, yellow, mint in rotation.
- Bar i (0-based) starts at 0.15 + 0.12·i s and grows from 0 to full height in 0.5 s with backOut (s = 2.6), anchored at the baseline, overshooting and settling.
- White dot (radius 6.5, 16 px above the bar top): starts 0.34 s after its bar with spring 1 − e^(−7t)·cos(16t).
- Exit: bars shrink with cubic ease-in over 0.26 s starting at 1.78 + 0.045·i s; dots vanish first, over 0.2 s from 1.70 + 0.045·i s.
