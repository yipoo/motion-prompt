Implement "Image zoom on hover", Motion Prompt #082.

What the sample shows: The photo under the cursor slowly enlarges while its frame stays the same size, and shrinks back when the cursor leaves.

Motion spec:
- Two photo frames, 222 × 118 (radius 8). The cursor rests on the left one, then the right one, for about 1.5 s each.
- On hover the content scales 1→1.15 from the centre over 0.5 s (cubic ease-out); on leave it returns over 0.5 s.
- Anything outside the frame is clipped (overflow: hidden). The frame and the caption do not move.

When to use it: Use it on photo grids (work, products, articles) to show which item is about to be chosen.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 539 × 208 px; loop: 4.8 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
