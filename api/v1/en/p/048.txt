Implement "L-cut", Motion Prompt #048.

What the sample shows: The previous scene's audio carries on for a while after the picture has changed; the picture is shown on top with video and audio timelines below.

Motion spec:

- Playhead: moves left to right at constant speed over 3.6 s (0.4 s to 4.0 s); picture switches at 55% (about 2.38 s), audio at 75% (about 3.10 s)
- In the offset section (55–75%) the picture is already B while the audio is still A; the section is marked with a pale blue band and two blue dashed lines
- Layout: the top 134 px is the picture (fixed camera, subjects do not move); the bottom 74 px holds two timeline rows, video and audio (A grey, B pale blue, waveform on the audio row)
- Labels: "Video A / Video B" (video A / B) at bottom left of the picture; the audio now playing at bottom right ("♪ Audio A" grey → "♪ Audio B" blue)
- Return: cross-fade back to the start over the last 0.5 s (one pass = 4.7 s)
