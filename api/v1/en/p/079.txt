Implement "Scroll progress bar", Motion Prompt #079.

What the sample shows: As the article is read a bit at a time, the line along the top grows from the left and reaches the far end at the bottom of the page.

Motion spec:
- The article scrolls 0→430 px in four 110 px steps, each moving for 0.45 s then pausing 0.4 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- It returns 430→0 at 3.75–4.55 s.
- A 6 px bar along the top of the viewport: light grey track, orange fill.
- Fill width = 511 px × scroll / 430, the scroll fraction itself (the same as CSS animation-timeline: scroll()).

When to use it: Use it on long articles to show how much is left to read.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 539 × 208 px; loop: 4.8 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
