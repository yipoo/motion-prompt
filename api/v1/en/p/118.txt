Implement "Film grain", Motion Prompt #118.

What the sample shows: Fine grain appears over the whole of the same landscape and the colours lean slightly towards a warm cream.

Motion spec:
- Scene 3: a bust on the left (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px) and hills, a house, trees and the sun (x 432, y 54, radius 15 px) on the right. 
- Grain: 2,600 squares (fixed seed), 0.8–1.7 px; 55% dark #3A3530, 45% white
- Grain opacity: 16–38% × the effect; the grain does not move (no flicker)
- Colour: every colour moves 16% towards #F2DDBE
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))

When to use it: To remove the clean digital look and add an analogue film texture.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 539 × 208 px; loop: 5 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
