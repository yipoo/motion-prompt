Implement "Particles", Motion Prompt #015.

What the sample shows: Particles keep shooting up from an emitter, spread out and fall.

Motion spec:
- 84 particles, emitted from the bottom centre (horizontal centre, 20 px above the bottom edge).
- Initial velocity: ±190 px/s horizontally, 250–400 px/s upward. Gravity 470 px/s² (parabolic paths).
- Each particle repeats with a period of 2.35 s or about 1.57 s, with a random start offset (fixed seed, so every run looks the same).
- Lifetime up to 1.5 s: fade in over the first 0.06 s, fade out over the last 30%. Scale 1.15→0.7.
- Shapes are circles, squares and triangles (size 5–11 px) in blue, coral, yellow and mint, spinning at up to ±4.5 rad/s.

When to use it: Use it to add celebration or liveliness, in place of confetti or sparks.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 539 × 208 px; loop: 4.7 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
