Implement "Parallax scrolling", Motion Prompt #073.

What the sample shows: As the page scrolls down, the headline moves up normally while the sky and sun, far hills and near hills move more slowly.

Motion spec:
- A browser window with a 511 × 168 viewport. Scroll: 0→120 px at 0.3–1.9 s, 120→0 at 2.9–4.3 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- Each layer moves by scroll × factor: sky and sun 0.15, far hills 0.4, near hills 0.7.
- Headline (28 px), button and the next section move at 1.0 (normal scrolling).
- The next section (white) starts at the bottom of the hero (168 px) and slides over the slower background.
- The scrollbar thumb on the right moves with the scroll.

When to use it: Use it to give the hero or a section photo a sense of depth.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 539 × 208 px; loop: 4.8 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
