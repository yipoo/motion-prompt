Implement "Carousel", Motion Prompt #058.

What the sample shows: Each click on the right arrow slides a row of three different images one step to the left, and the dot below moves.

Motion spec:
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. Slides 330×138, radius 8, centred with an 18 px gap; the neighbours peek in at the edges.
- White round buttons (radius 18, shadow blur 10) with chevrons at left and right; three dots below (current one blue 16×8, others 8×8).
- The cursor clicks the right arrow (514, 88) at 1.0, 2.1 and 3.2 s.
- Each click moves the row 348 px left (one slide plus gap) over 0.4 s with easeInOutCubic.
- The three pictures (landscape, three circles, bar chart) repeat, so after three moves the first is back.

When to use it: Use it to show several images or featured items in limited space, one at a time.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
Canvas: 542 × 191 px; loop: 4.5 seconds. Preserve the reference shapes, timing and easing; adapt text, colours and assets only. Support responsive layouts and prefers-reduced-motion.
