Implement "Ripple effect", Motion Prompt #085.

What the sample shows: Each click sends a white circle out from the click point to fill the button, then it fades away.

Motion spec:
- Button 280 × 56 (orange, radius 10). Clicks at 0.7 s (left of centre) and 2.9 s (right of centre).
- The circle is centred on the click point; its radius grows from 0 to the distance to the button's farthest corner over 0.6 s (cubic ease-out).
- White at 0.4 opacity, fading to 0 between 0.3 and 0.8 s after the click.
- The circle is clipped to the button (overflow: hidden); the label sits above it.

When to use it: Use it to confirm a press on a button or list item right where it happened.
