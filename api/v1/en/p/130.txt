Implement "Wave text", Motion Prompt #130.

What the sample shows: The letters of "WAVE TEXT" rise and fall one after another, like a wave.

Motion spec:
- "WAVE TEXT" (70 px, bold, 4 px letter spacing). Three waves per 4.7 s (1.567 s per wave).
- Vertical offset of letter i = −28·sin(φ − 0.62i), with φ = 2π × 3 × time ÷ 4.7 (each letter lags the previous by 0.62 rad).
- Rising letters stretch vertically (1 + 0.06·s) and shift from white towards yellow (s is the sine value, clamped to 0–1 for the colour).
- A shadow ellipse under each letter shrinks as the letter rises.

When to use it: Use it on a logo, a short heading or a caption timed to music, to add fun and rhythm.

Language: English, including text inside animated demos. Bundle assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2 (Motion Sans) for Chinese and multilingual glyphs; do not depend on installed or remote fonts.
