Implement "Cube transition", Motion Prompt #139.

What the sample shows: Like a turning cube, the front face swings away and the next face comes round.

Motion spec:
- Face A (an "A" on coral) and face B (a "B" on mint), each 250 × 146. 2.35 s each, A→B→A.
- Rotation 0→90° at 0.45–1.6 s (1.15 s), cubic ease-in-out, around a vertical axis through the centre of the cube (depth equal to the face width, 250).
- Perspective: camera distance 820 px. Each face is drawn as 2 px vertical slices, each sized and placed by its perspective factor.
- The face turning away darkens up to 55% (0.55·sinφ); the incoming face starts 55% dark and brightens (0.55·cosφ). The floor shadow follows the visible width.

When to use it: Use it to show products, plans or screens one after another with a sense of depth.
