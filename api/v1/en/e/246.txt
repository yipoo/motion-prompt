Motion Prompt #246 · Radial contour morph
A mint circle becomes an four-lobed scalloped contour and returns.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Contour centre (269,104), 161 angular samples, initial radius 63 px.
- Interpolate radius to alternating 75/56 px bands using q; fill mint and outline 4 px.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 06
  CELLS[5]=wrap((k,p)=>{let q=wave(p);k.beginPath();for(let i=0;i<=160;i++){let a=i/160*TAU,r=63*(1-q)+q*(i%40<20?75:56),x=269+r*Math.cos(a),y=104+r*Math.sin(a);i?k.lineTo(x,y):k.moveTo(x,y);}k.closePath();k.fillStyle=C.mint;k.fill();k.strokeStyle=C.ink;k.lineWidth=4;k.stroke();});
