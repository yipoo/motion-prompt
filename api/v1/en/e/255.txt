Motion Prompt #255 · Pixel mosaic
A deterministic coloured mosaic builds up in scattered order, then dissolves.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Use 20×8 squares of 17 px, with 19 px grid spacing.
- Delay = ((7x+11y) mod 23)/23; opacity = clamp(5(q−delay)); cycle blue, mint and orange.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 15
