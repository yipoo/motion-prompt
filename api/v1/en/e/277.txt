Motion Prompt #277 · Gravity bounce
A ball follows repeated parabolic flights and squashes on contact with the floor.

- Two flights occur per cycle: p = (2t/4.8) mod 1 and center y = 164−123·4p(1−p), x = 269.
- The base radius is 17 px; vertical scale s = 1−0.22|2p−1|^18 and horizontal scale 1/s preserve area during impact squash.
- The floor is at y = 181; the shadow horizontal radius is 24−11·4p(1−p) px and narrows at the flight peak.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
