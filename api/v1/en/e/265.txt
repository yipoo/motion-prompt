Motion Prompt #265 · Perspective flip
A card turns around its vertical axis and reveals its reverse.

- A 152 × 106 px card rotates by a = 2πt/4.8 around its vertical axis; the projection uses focal distance 360 px and center (269.5,109).
- The face is blue when cos(a) ≥ 0 and coral otherwise; FRONT/BACK labels compress horizontally by |cos(a)|.
- A ground shadow at y = 177 changes its horizontal radius from 12 to 84 px with the rotation; a full revolution closes the loop.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 01
  CELLS[0] = scene('PERSPECTIVE FLIP', '透视翻卡', a => {
    const theta = a, corners = [[-76,-53,0],[76,-53,0],[76,53,0],[-76,53,0]].map(p => project(...rotate(p,theta)));
    ellipse(269.5, 177, 72 * Math.abs(Math.cos(a)) + 12, 6, '#080e1c', true);
    poly(corners, Math.cos(a) >= 0 ? C.blue : C.coral, C.ink);
    const s = Math.abs(Math.cos(a)); X.save(); X.translate(269.5,109); X.scale(Math.max(.001,s),1); text(Math.cos(a)>=0?'FRONT':'BACK',Math.cos(a)>=0?'正面':'背面',0,0,24,C.bg,'center'); X.restore();
  });
