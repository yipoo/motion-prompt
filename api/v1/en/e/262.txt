Motion Prompt #262 · Approval stamp
An approval stamp grows and tilts with a radiating burst.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Stamp frame is 244×96 px with 5 px orange outline and 36 px title.
- Scale 0.6+0.4q, angle −0.2+0.4q rad; twelve surrounding rays expand by up to 33 px.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
