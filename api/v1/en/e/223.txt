Motion Prompt #223 · Radar expansion
A six-axis radar polygon expands from the center.

- Center (265,120); hexagonal guides have radii 25, 50, 75 px; target ratios are .9, .65, .82, .5, .75, .95.
- Vertex radii increase with cubic ease-out over 0.35–2.85 s; blue outline and pale-blue fill.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
