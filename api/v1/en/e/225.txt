Motion Prompt #225 · Flip counter
A four-digit order counter rises with folding shadows on digit cards.

- Four dark 68×103 px digit cards use a 79 px horizontal step and middle dividers.
- Cubic ease-out counts 0000→2480 over 0.35–2.85 s; folding shadows decay as |sin(9u−0.6i)| × (1−p).
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
