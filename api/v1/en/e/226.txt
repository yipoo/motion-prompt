Motion Prompt #226 · Odometer
Five digit reels roll upward in a clipped window and settle on 12345.

- Frame 363×107 px, five white digit windows, digit row spacing 78 px, font size 54 px.
- The value eases to 12345 over 0.35–2.85 s; integer and fractional parts of each decimal place determine reel positions.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
