Motion Prompt #218 · Line reveal
A line reveals from left to right with a coral leading dot.

- Eight points are spaced 62 px apart; their heights equal a fixed series × 120 px above y = 180.
- A 451 px clipping window opens with cubic ease-out over 0.35–2.85 s; the leading dot has radius 7 px.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
