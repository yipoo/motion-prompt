Motion Prompt #285 · Magnetic field
Light particles trace arcing field lines between two magnetic poles.

- Ten arcs connect x = 213 to 325 around y = 112: y = 112±h sin(πp), with h = 27,40,53,66,79.
- Each arc has three mint particles of radius 2.7 px, with p = (t/4.8+k/3+0.06j) mod 1.
- The N and S poles are 57 × 28 px rectangles; they cover the particle wrap points so the motion loops without a visible jump.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
