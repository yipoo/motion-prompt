Motion Prompt #258 · Circular type
Letters orbit a circle while staying tangent to the ring.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Place glyphs evenly around radius 76 px at (269,104), using 19 px type.
- Rotate glyph positions one turn per cycle; rotate each glyph by its angle plus π/2.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
