Motion Prompt #249 · Iris reveal
A circular aperture reveals a landscape around a central title.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Clip the landscape with a circle centred at (269,104).
- Aperture radius is 220q px; the 24 px title stays visible above the mask.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
