Motion Prompt #243 · Orbit dots
Five dots orbit an ellipse, with one orange leader.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Orbit centre (269,104), radii 170×61 px; track width 3 px.
- Five radius-9 px dots are equally spaced; each completes one turn in 4.8 s.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
