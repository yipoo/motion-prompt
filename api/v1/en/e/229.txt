Motion Prompt #229 · Segmented progress
Twelve progress blocks fill sequentially and show check marks.

- Twelve 29×34 px blocks use a 36 px horizontal step; completed blocks are blue with white checks.
- Block i fills with cubic ease-out over 0.3 + 0.17i to 0.65 + 0.17i s; the bottom count reaches 12 over 0.35–2.85 s.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
