Motion Prompt #271 · Orbital cards
Six numbered cards circle a glowing center, passing in front and behind.

- Six cards use q = a+i·2π/6, x = 269+172 cos(q), z = 90 sin(q), y = 110−0.37z.
- Cards are sorted far to near and scale by 1−z/260 from a 52 × 62 px base size.
- The static orbit has radii 184 × 51 px; the center glows with radius 10 px and all cards orbit once per 4.8 s.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 07
  CELLS[6] = scene('ORBITAL CARDS', '轨道卡片', a => {
    ellipse(269,110,184,51,C.grid); const cards=[];for(let i=0;i<6;i++){const q=a+i*TAU/6;cards.push({q,i,z:Math.sin(q)*90});}cards.sort((a,b)=>b.z-a.z).forEach(c=>{const scale=1-c.z/260;miniCard(269+Math.cos(c.q)*172,110-c.z*.37,52*scale,62*scale,[C.blue,C.mint,C.coral][c.i%3],c.i+1);});
    glow(269,110,10,C.gold);
  });
