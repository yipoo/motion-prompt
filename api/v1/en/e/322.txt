Motion Prompt #322 · Velocity marquee
A marquee accelerates and tilts with its instantaneous travel speed. The canvas is a deterministic motion demonstration, not a connected control.

- Translate a repeated 450px unit with position 450p + 60 sin(2πp) over 4800ms; derive skew from instantaneous velocity. Connect to smoothed scroll velocity in production, clamp skew, and stop under reduced motion.
- Reference canvas: 539×208 CSS pixels; preview cycle 4800ms. Scale uniformly for other sizes.
- Keep motion deterministic when scrubbing. Honor reduced motion with a stable keyframe, preserve readable labels, and do not depend on color alone.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
