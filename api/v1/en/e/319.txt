Motion Prompt #319 · Elastic volume feedback
A slider track stretches and compresses as its thumb approaches the upper bound. The canvas is a deterministic motion demonstration, not a connected control.

- Use a 216×38px track; drive the thumb on a 2400ms cycle and map the final 15% to 18px track extension and 4.5px height compression.
- Reference canvas: 539×208 CSS pixels; preview cycle 4800ms. Scale uniformly for other sizes.
- Keep motion deterministic when scrubbing. Honor reduced motion with a stable keyframe, preserve readable labels, and do not depend on color alone.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
