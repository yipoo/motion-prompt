Motion Prompt #232 · Liquid level
Liquid rises inside a circular vessel while its surface gently waves.

- Vessel center (270,120), radius 72 px; surface amplitude 5 px with wave sin(0.07x−4u).
- Cubic ease-out over 0.35–2.85 s raises the surface from y = 192 to 58; the number increases 0%→93%.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 16
  CELLS[15]=draw('液位占比','Liquid level',(u,p)=>{X.save();X.beginPath();X.arc(270,120,72,0,Math.PI*2);X.clip();rect(190,40,160,160,'#e2e9e2',0);const y=192-134*p;let pts=[[190,200],[350,200]];for(let x=350;x>=190;x-=4)pts.push([x,y+5*Math.sin(x*.07-u*4)]);poly(pts,C.mint,true);X.restore();arc(270,120,72,0,Math.PI*2,C.white,4);text(`${Math.round(p*93)}%`,270,120,32,C.ink,'center',700);});
