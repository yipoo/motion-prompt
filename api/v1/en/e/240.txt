Motion Prompt #240 · Live data stream
A two-frequency waveform streams horizontally while its live readout changes.

- 81 samples use a 5.6 px horizontal step; y = 122 + 30sin(a) + 15sin(2a), a = i/12 + 2u.
- The coral endpoint has radius 6 px; the live readout is round(120 + 30sin(2u)), updating throughout the cycle.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
