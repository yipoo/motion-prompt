Motion Prompt #261 · Glyph wave
Four coloured characters bob in a staggered sine wave.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Four 48 px characters start at x=165 with 65 px spacing.
- Glyph i uses y=104+35 sin(2πt/4.8+0.9i); colours blue, orange, mint and ink.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
