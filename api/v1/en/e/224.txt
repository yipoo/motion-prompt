Motion Prompt #224 · Waterfall accumulation
Positive and negative changes appear in sequence, linked at cumulative levels.

- Six changes are 38, 25, −18, 32, −12, 22; scale 1.35 px per unit, bar width 43 px.
- Item i grows over 0.3 + 0.22i to 1.3 + 0.22i s; positives are mint, negatives coral.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
