Motion Prompt #253 · Split shutters
Eight vertical image shutters enter from alternating top and bottom directions.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Divide the 380×148 px image into eight 47.5 px columns.
- Alternate masks move ±160(1−q) px vertically; q peaks halfway through the cycle.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
