Motion Prompt #270 · Depth parallax
Landscape layers slide by different amounts to reveal depth.

- Three mountain layers shift horizontally by 8 sin(a),23 sin(a),50 sin(a) px; their baseline heights are 113,143,179.
- Five foreground trees are spaced 110 px apart and shift 65 sin(a) px; each canopy radius is 12 px.
- The sun has radius 20 px and shifts only 8 sin(a) px at y = 62; every layer returns smoothly after 4.8 s.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
