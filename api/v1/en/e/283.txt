Motion Prompt #283 · Particle shape morph
A ring of glowing points morphs into a heart and back.

- One hundred twenty points sample q = 2πi/120; the source circle has radius 68 px around (269,112).
- Heart coordinates are x = 80 sin³(q), y = −5(13 cos(q)−5 cos(2q)−2 cos(3q)−cos(4q)).
- Circle and heart coordinates interpolate by p = 0.5−0.5 cos(a); each point has radius 2 px and coral or gold glow.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 19
  CELLS[18] = scene('PARTICLE SHAPE MORPH', '粒子形状变换', a => {
    const p=.5-.5*Math.cos(a);for(let i=0;i<120;i++){const q=i*TAU/120,cx=68*Math.cos(q),cy=68*Math.sin(q),hx=5*16*Math.pow(Math.sin(q),3),hy=-5*(13*Math.cos(q)-5*Math.cos(2*q)-2*Math.cos(3*q)-Math.cos(4*q));glow(269+mix(cx,hx,p),112+mix(cy,hy,p),2,i%3?C.coral:C.gold);}text('CIRCLE ↔ HEART','圆形 ↔ 爱心',269,190,12,C.muted,'center');
  });
