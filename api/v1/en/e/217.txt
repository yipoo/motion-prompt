Motion Prompt #217 · Staggered bars
Six bars grow from a shared baseline with staggered starts.

- Bars are 42 px wide, spaced 71 px apart, with heights 68, 110, 88, 130, 98, 142 px.
- Bar i starts at 0.3 + 0.16i s and ends at 1.6 + 0.16i s using cubic ease-out.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
