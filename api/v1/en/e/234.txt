Motion Prompt #234 · Conversion funnel
Four funnel tiers reveal in sequence, showing decreasing conversion shares.

- Tier top widths are 420, 330, 240, 150 px; each is 28 px high with 35 px vertical steps; bottom edges are 60 px narrower.
- Tier i reveals over 0.3 + 0.25i to 1.1 + 0.25i s; after 70% progress it shows labels 100%, 76%, 52%, 29%.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
