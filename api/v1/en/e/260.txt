Motion Prompt #260 · Split type
The upper and lower halves of a word slide in opposite directions.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Use a 54 px headline centred on y=104; clip separate halves above and below y=104.
- Upper half moves −80q px in blue; lower half moves +80q px in orange.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
