Motion Prompt #239 · Network connections
Links draw between six nodes as data dots propagate along them.

- Six nodes and seven blue links; link i extends with cubic ease-out over 0.25 + .18i to 1 + .18i s.
- Coral radius-4 px dots traverse links every 2 s; white node radii pulse as 14 + 2sin(3u−i).
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
