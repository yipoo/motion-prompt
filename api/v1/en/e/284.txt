Motion Prompt #284 · Comet trail
A bright comet circles a gold sun while a fading tail follows its curved route.

- The comet follows x = 269+183 cos(a), y = 113+61 sin(a), completing one orbit in 4.8 s.
- Forty-three trail dots sample phase a−0.035i; opacity is (1−i/43)² and radius is 1+6(1−i/43) px.
- The leading nine trail dots are white and the rest blue; the comet head radius is 7 px and the central gold sun radius is 15 px.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
