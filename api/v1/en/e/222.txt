Motion Prompt #222 · Scatter clustering
Scattered points converge into three colored clusters.

- 42 dots of radius 5 px group by index modulo 3; target centers are 145 px apart horizontally.
- Cubic ease-out interpolates toward deterministic targets over 0.35–2.85 s; three reference circles have radius 40 px.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.
