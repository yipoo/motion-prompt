Motion Prompt #268 · Isometric layers
Five isometric plates separate vertically and come back together.

- Five diamond tops share x coordinates 180,269,359; each top is 179 px wide and 70 px tall.
- Layer separation is 10 + 10(1−cos(a)) px, ranging from 10 to 30; layer i is positioned at y = 158−i·separation.
- Every slab has a dark 7 px side face; five colors identify layers and a faint vertical guide stays at x = 269.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 04
  CELLS[3] = scene('ISOMETRIC LAYERS', '等距分层', a => {

    for(let i=0;i<5;i++){const y=158-i*spread, col=[C.blue,C.mint,C.coral,C.gold,C.ink][i]; const top=[[180,y],[269,y-35],[359,y],[269,y+35]];poly([[180,y],[269,y+35],[359,y],[359,y+7],[269,y+42],[180,y+7]],'#263b62');poly(top,col,C.bg); }
    line([[269,38],[269,185]],'#ffffff22');
  });
