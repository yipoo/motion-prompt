Motion Prompt #288 · Orbital particles
Twelve particles circle a luminous nucleus on three crossing elliptical orbits.

- Three ellipses with radii 108 × 27 px are rotated by 0,π/3,2π/3 around (269,113).
- Each ellipse carries four particles spaced π/2 apart, with phase q = a+0.7j+kπ/2; all complete one orbit per 4.8 s.
- Particles with sin(q) < 0 draw behind the radius-14 gold nucleus at radius 3 px; front particles draw last with radius 4.5 px and glow.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

    particles.filter(p=>p.z<0).forEach(p=>dot(p.x,p.y,3,p.c));glow(269,113,14,C.gold);particles.filter(p=>p.z>=0).forEach(p=>glow(p.x,p.y,4.5,p.c));
  });
