Motion Prompt #238 · Packet transfer
Packets continuously move right through three transfer channels.

- Three lanes are spaced 47 px vertically; each carries four 18×12 px packets between 53×32 px nodes.
- Packets move uniformly along 300 px paths with a 2.4 s period; lane phases differ by 0.15.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
