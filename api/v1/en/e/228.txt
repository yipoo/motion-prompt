Motion Prompt #228 · Progress ring
A blue progress arc grows around a percentage to 86%.

- Center (270,119), radius 64 px, arc width 13 px, starting at the top at −π/2.
- Cubic ease-out grows to 86% over 0.35–2.85 s; the center shows the matching integer percentage.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.
