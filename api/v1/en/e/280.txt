Motion Prompt #280 · Elastic collision
Two balls approach, touch and rebound symmetrically with a brief contact flash.

- Both balls have radius 22 px and center y = 126; center separation is 44+126(0.5+0.5 cos(a)) px.
- The centers are symmetric about x = 269; minimum separation is 44 px at t = 2.4 s, so their edges touch without overlap.
- Eight gold contact rays span radii 28–42 px and use opacity max(0,−cos(a))^30; the floor is at y = 153.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

    text('Approach · contact · rebound','靠近 · 接触 · 回弹',269,183,12,C.muted,'center');
  });
