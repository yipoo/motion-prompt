Motion Prompt #193 · Ripple feedback
A circular ripple expands from the click point.

- Canvas 539 × 208; 4.8-second loop; background #eff3e9, primary #597a36; bundled Motion Sans.
- A 200 × 66 button at (170,72); ripple centre (235,105). From 0.6–1.5 s radius grows 0→180 and opacity falls 0.6→0, clipped inside the button.
- Drawing depends only on time. Text follows Chinese/English language selection; reduced motion uses a static keyframe.
