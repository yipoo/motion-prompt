Motion Prompt #248 · Breathing spiral
An orange three-turn spiral expands, contracts and rotates.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Use 260 points for three turns centred at (269,104).
- Radius grows from 8 to 43+43q px; rotation is one turn per 4.8 s, stroke width 4 px.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
