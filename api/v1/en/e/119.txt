Motion Prompt #119 · Light leak
From the right edge of the same landscape a band of leaked light comes in, pale yellow on the inside and red at the edge, swaying slowly.

- Scene 3: a bust on the left (head centre x 140, y 82, radius 28 px) and hills, a house, trees and the sun (x 432, y 54, radius 15 px) on the right.
- Shape: four tall ellipses centred 30 px outside the right edge (swaying ±6 px over the loop), vertical radius 1.1× the frame height
- Horizontal radius and colour: 250 px #FFD089 at 35%, 170 px #FF9A3D at 40%, 95 px #FF5A3A at 45%, 45 px #FF3B30 at 50% (each grows from 0 to this radius)
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 5s.
