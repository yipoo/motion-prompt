Motion Prompt #256 · Diagonal wipe
A slanted orange divider sweeps across a landscape reveal.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Top edge position x=80+380q; the bottom lags by 70 sin(πq) px.
- Clip the landscape to the left of the divider and outline the divider with a 4 px orange line.
