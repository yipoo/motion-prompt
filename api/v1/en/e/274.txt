Motion Prompt #274 · Elastic rope
A suspended rope undulates between fixed anchors while its center weight rides the wave.

- The rope uses 101 points from x = 80 to 459; both ends remain at y = 61.
- At normalized position p, y = 61+70 sin(πp)+22 sin(4πp−a) sin(πp), producing sag plus a traveling wave.
- The mint rope is 4 px wide, white anchors have radius 6 px, and a glowing coral weight of radius 14 px follows the midpoint.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
