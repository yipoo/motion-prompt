Motion Prompt #221 · Heatmap scan
Grid colors reveal column by column while a scan line moves right.

- A 5×12 grid uses 29×21 px cells with horizontal/vertical steps of 35/27 px.
- Column c colors in over 0.25 + 0.1c to 1.15 + 0.1c s; the scan line moves 385 px over 4.8 s.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.
