Motion Prompt #316 · Sliding tile loader
Three tiles take turns moving into the empty corner of a square. The canvas is a deterministic motion demonstration, not a connected control.

- Place three 27px tiles on four 38px-pitch slots; move one tile into the empty slot every 400ms with cubic ease-out.
- Reference canvas: 539×208 CSS pixels; preview cycle 4800ms. Scale uniformly for other sizes.
- Keep motion deterministic when scrubbing. Honor reduced motion with a stable keyframe, preserve readable labels, and do not depend on color alone.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 04
