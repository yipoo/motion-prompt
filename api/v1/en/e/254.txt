Motion Prompt #254 · Rotating slit
A rotating narrow window scans a stationary landscape.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- A centred rectangle mask is 420q px wide and 38 px tall.
- Rotate the mask once per cycle while counter-rotating image coordinates to keep the landscape still.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
