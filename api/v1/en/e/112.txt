Motion Prompt #112 · Vignette
The frame darkens smoothly from the corners while the person and the road in the middle stay bright.

- Scene 2: a street corner; buildings, trees and street lamps (x 114, 292, 494) behind a road at y 132–162; the person stands front left (feet at x 176, y 200, 124 px tall).
- Shape: an ellipse centred on the frame (vertical radius = horizontal × 208/539 × 1.35)
- Clear out to 45% of the 334 px horizontal radius, then rgb(32,35,43) rising from 0% to 70% towards the edge
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 5s.

  // @element 16
    CELLS[15] = cell((k, u) => {
    const a = env(u);
    stageStreet(k, BASE);
    k.save();
    k.translate(CW / 2, CH / 2);
    k.scale(1, CH / CW * 1.35);
    const g = k.createRadialGradient(0, 0, CW * .45 * .62, 0, 0, CW * .62);
    g.addColorStop(0, "rgba(32,35,43,0)");
    g.addColorStop(1, `rgba(32,35,43,${(.7 * a).toFixed(3)})`);
    k.fillStyle = g;
    k.fillRect(-CW, -CW, CW * 2, CW * 2);
    k.restore();
  });
