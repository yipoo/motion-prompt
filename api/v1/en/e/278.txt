Motion Prompt #278 · Coupled springs
Two wheeled masses oscillate in opposite directions between three elastic springs.

- Mass centers are x₁ = 205+24 sin(a) and x₂ = 334−24 sin(a), both at y = 125.
- Blocks are 42 × 42 px with 9 px corners; three springs join the block edges to each other and walls at x = 86 and 453.
- Each spring uses 12 turns sampled at 48 segments; outer amplitude is 7 px and central amplitude 9 px, tapered to zero at the endpoints.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
