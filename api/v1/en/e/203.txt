Motion Prompt #203 · Sliding radio indicator
A selected dot travels between three radio options.

- Canvas 539 × 208; 4.8-second loop; background #eff3e9, primary #597a36; bundled Motion Sans.
- A selected dot travels between three radio options. Cubic ease-out enters from 0.4–1.4 s and reverses from 3.5–4.4 s.
- Drawing depends only on time. Text follows Chinese/English language selection; reduced motion uses a static keyframe.
