Motion Prompt #235 · Ranking race
Four bars grow and exchange ranked positions.

- Four items keep fixed colors; initial rows [0,1,2,3] move to [2,0,3,1] with 34 px row spacing.
- Cubic ease-out over 0.7–3.2 s changes widths from 350×.35 to 350×[.82,.68,.95,.74] px; labels update in sync.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
