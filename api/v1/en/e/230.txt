Motion Prompt #230 · Market ticker
Prices update in stages while the label switches between buying pressure and a pullback.

- Prices are 102.8, 108.4, 105.2, 114.6; stages advance every 1.1 s and interpolate with 0.45 s cubic ease-out.
- The main number is 48 px; mint labels indicate rises, coral indicates falls; 16 small bars illuminate over time.
- Canvas 539×208, cycle 4.8 s; all content fades in over 0–0.2 s and out over 4.5–4.8 s, leaving the same background at both ends.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.

  // @element 14
  CELLS[13]=draw('行情跳动','Market ticker',(u)=>{const prices=[102.8,108.4,105.2,114.6],i=Math.min(3,Math.floor(u/1.1)),q=progress(u,i*1.1,i*1.1+.45),v=i?prices[i-1]+(prices[i]-prices[i-1])*q:prices[0];text(`$ ${v.toFixed(2)}`,270,103,48,C.ink,'center',700);const up=i===0||prices[i]>prices[i-1];text(t(up?'▲ 买盘上升':'▼ 短线回调',up?'▲ BUY PRESSURE':'▼ PULLBACK'),270,151,14,up?C.mint:C.coral,'center');for(let j=0;j<16;j++){let h=16+(j*13%40);rect(59+j*27,186-h,16,h,j<=u*3.5?C.mint:C.line);}});
