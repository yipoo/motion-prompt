Motion Prompt #250 · Vertical blinds
Ten vertical strips open horizontally to reveal a landscape.

- 539×208 px Canvas 2D card; 4.8 s deterministic seamless loop. Unless stated otherwise q=(1−cos(2πt/4.8))/2.
- Landscape bounds (80,30), 380×148 px; divide it into ten 38 px columns.
- Each column reveals width 38q px from its left edge, then closes in the second half.

Canvas: 539×208; cycle: 4.8s.
